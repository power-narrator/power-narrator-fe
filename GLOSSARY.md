# Power Narrator

Power Narrator turns sections of a presentation's speaker notes into narration audio.

## Language

**Narration section**:
An ordered portion of a slide's speaker notes with narration text and a speaker selection. Each section can have its own narration audio.
_Avoid_: Segment

**Section audio**:
The narration audio associated with one narration section on a slide.

**Play Across Slides**:
A section audio playback setting that is either enabled or disabled. When enabled, the audio can continue as the presentation advances to subsequent slides.
